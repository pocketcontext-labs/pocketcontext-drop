# Drop 0.7.0

Visitors select their executable before entering an email address. Drop tells them immediately whether they can sign in and continue or join the verified waitlist. Invited users deploy compatible Linux executables with settings from their Drop manifest. Executables without a manifest use the advanced deployment form.

Each account receives 60 awake minutes shared by its apps. Sleeping is free. During the trial, apps use local disk and have no platform S3. Trial expiry archives the apps and retains their volumes. An account can buy a simulated 500-credit pack for €5. Compute costs one credit per awake hour per size multiplier. Platform S3 costs the configured AWS cost plus 25%. A paid account that runs out of credits has its apps suspended until a top-up. The account disk allowance is 10 GiB, including archived volumes.

An owner or administrator can permanently delete an app by typing its slug. Drop waits for node acknowledgement before removing the app’s S3 versions, secrets and unshared releases. Events and ledger entries remain available for audit.
