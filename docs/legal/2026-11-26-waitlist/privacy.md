# Drop Waitlist Privacy Notice

Version of 26 November 2026

Alberto Miorin, trading as PocketContext, collects your email address directly from you to verify your request, manage the waitlist and send an invitation if a place becomes available. Administrators can see your verified email, the date you joined, invitation status and mail delivery information. We keep a record of the terms version you accepted and when you verified the address.

We process the request to take steps at your request before entering a hosting agreement under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. We limit verification attempts and process delivery and security records in our legitimate interest in preventing abuse under Article 6(1)(f). Joining the waitlist does not subscribe you to marketing.

Verification codes expire. You can withdraw through the waitlist page or contact us to request removal. We use waitlist information while handling your request. After activation we process account and security records under the Drop Beta Privacy Policy. Backup copies may persist until the applicable backup cycle ends.

Resend delivers verification and invitation messages and processes email data in the United States. See the [Drop Beta Privacy Policy](#/legal/2026-11-26/privacy) for the service providers and transfer safeguards, your rights and how to complain to a supervisory authority.
