Version of 26 November 2026

## Accounts and hosting

We receive an invited person's email address from the person issuing the invitation. We use it to check access, deliver sign-in messages and identify the verified owner of a deployed Crew app. We store account roles, quotas, sessions, the accepted terms version and its acceptance time. The reader stores a session credential in your browser. An email address is necessary to activate an account.

We process uploaded executables and their hashes, deployment settings, app databases, attachments and replicas to run the apps you deploy. The service records deployment events, operational reports and usage figures to allocate resources, diagnose failures and protect accounts. Service providers handling requests can process technical information such as IP addresses and request times. Your app may process additional data under its operator's instructions. Contact the app operator about how that app uses your information.

We process account and hosting data to provide the service you request under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. We process invitations, operational records and security information in our legitimate interests in controlling access, keeping the service available and preventing abuse under Article 6(1)(f). Where a legal duty requires processing, Article 6(1)(c) applies.

## Providers and transfers

Hetzner supplies hosting infrastructure. Amazon Web Services supplies storage, replica storage, secret management and usage reporting. Cloudflare routes public traffic. Resend delivers sign-in email. We share information with these providers as needed for those functions. The Beta deployment uses European hosting and AWS storage in Stockholm. Resend processes message content and delivery records in the United States. Its published safeguards include the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and EU standard contractual clauses. Cloudflare’s customer agreement provides for EU standard contractual clauses for restricted transfers. You can request a copy of the safeguards applicable to your data from Alberto Miorin. [Resend transfer information](https://resend.com/security/gdpr/) · [Cloudflare customer data processing addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/)

## Retention and deletion

We retain active account information and app data while providing hosting. Archiving stops the app and preserves its volume, releases and replicas for restoration. Contact us to request deletion. We assess which records can be removed, including whether any must be retained for legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of claims. Shared executable storage can retain identical bytes required by another deployment. Backup and replica copies may remain until their retention cycles expire.

The configured defaults for replica storage are 30 days for superseded object versions, 90 days for storage access logs and 30 days for inventory reports. These periods apply to those storage records. Closing a browser session or archiving an app does not start automatic deletion of the hosted account or current app content.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. We may need to verify your identity to act on a request. You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, including the authority where you live or work or the [Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information](https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de/).
